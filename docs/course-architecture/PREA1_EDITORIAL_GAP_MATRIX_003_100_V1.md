## Canonical acceptance contract

Every mission must have the same eight sections and exact counts: Explore 10 vocabulary pairs; Practice 20 autocorrectable tasks; Grammar 10 autocorrectable tasks; Pronunciation 10 cards (English, Spanish, Spanish-friendly guide, British audio); Reading one complete passage plus 10 questions; Writing 10 audio dictations with evaluation; Speaking 10 distinct prompts with model answers, recording and clearly labelled transcription feedback (not phonetic scoring); Listening one en-GB audio, visible passage with ten inline gaps, ten unique bank words and select/place/move/replace interaction.

Answer keys stay server-side. Browser TTS is a provisional playback strategy, not proof of a verified British voice or stored audio file. Legacy Listening MCQs are not equivalent to the canonical gap-fill.

## Required editorial workflow before conversion

1. Freeze mission learning objective, target vocabulary and grammar point.
2. For every source item, assign `keep`, `rewrite`, `replace` or `exclude`, with a reason.
3. Verify natural British English, accurate Spanish, age-appropriate context, one unambiguous answer and plausible distractors.
4. Author missing items to exact canonical counts. Do not repeat items merely to reach ten.
5. Create explicit Listening gap maps with ten unique bank entries and a one-to-one mapping to ten passage tokens.
6. Create ten Speaking prompt/model pairs; do not claim phonetic assessment from transcription similarity.
7. Store expected answers/rubrics only in protected server-side evaluator data.
8. Validate JSON Schema, repository validator and per-mission semantic checks before any UI conversion.
9. Convert one pilot from each structural family, test in browser, then batch-convert.
10. Keep each PR to one consolidated commit; do not merge or deploy without explicit approval.

## Scope and caveats

- This matrix covers the source inventory for missions 003–100. It is not an item-by-item editorial approval of every Grammar/Reading/Listening question.
- Standalone HTML and inline scripts are preserved in the extraction archives. Runtime-generated questions must be executed/reviewed separately before exact source answer mapping can be certified.
- Counts are taken from extracted source objects or visible static panels. A source panel or counter is not proof of ten distinct valid items.
- No canonical JSON, learner page, runtime, API or database has been changed in this phase.
