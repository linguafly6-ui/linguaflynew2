# Pre-A1 016 — Days of the Week

Updated grammar focus: **prepositions of time**.

- **ON** + days of the week: on Monday, on Friday, on Sundays.
- **IN** + parts of the day and seasons: in the morning, in winter.
- **AT** + clock times: at eight o’clock.

ON is the main target for this mission; IN and AT appear as a simple contrast. The ten grammar questions and protected server-side answer map are aligned.

The rest of the canonical mission remains unchanged. Browser speech synthesis uses en-GB; no stored audio asset or D1 attempt persistence was added.
