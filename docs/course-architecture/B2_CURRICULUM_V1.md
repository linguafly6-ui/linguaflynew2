# LinguaFly Academy — B2 Curriculum v1

Status: curriculum map approved for staged authoring; mission 001 is the first canonical pilot.
Target: CEFR B2, British English (en-GB).
Structure: 10 units × 10 missions = 100 missions.

## Course outcomes

By the end of B2, learners should be able to:
- understand the main ideas and relevant detail in complex texts on concrete and abstract topics;
- interact with sufficient fluency and spontaneity for regular interaction with proficient speakers;
- present clear, detailed descriptions and arguments, evaluate alternatives and support opinions with reasons and examples;
- write clear, structured texts such as reviews, reports, proposals and argumentative essays;
- use a broad vocabulary and a range of complex grammatical structures with increasing control.

The course prioritises productive, high-frequency B2 structures. Highly marked inversion, dense nominalisation and rare idiomatic patterns are enrichment rather than core pass requirements.

## Mission contract

Every complete mission follows the master Academy template:
1. Explore: exactly 10 vocabulary pairs plus concise grammar guidance.
2. Practice: exactly 20 varied, checkable tasks.
3. Grammar: exactly 10 auto-checkable items.
4. Pronunciation: exactly 10 word cards with approximate Spanish reading guidance and a clearly labelled audio source.
5. Reading: one complete passage and exactly 10 comprehension questions.
6. Writing: exactly 10 dictations, each containing multiple related sentences at B2, with three playback passes per segment.
7. Speaking: exactly 10 prompts with model answers; transcription is not represented as phonetic assessment.
8. Listening: one complete passage, 10 numbered gaps and a 10-word bank.
